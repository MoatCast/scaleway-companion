## Description (4000)
Free and open source. No ads, no subscriptions, no tracking.

Companion for Scaleway shows your Scaleway spending, invoices and Instances on your iPhone. It is read-only and never changes your cloud resources.

• This month's spending and an end-of-month forecast
• Monthly history and a breakdown by category
• Invoices, with PDF sharing
• Instances across projects and zones
• Budget alerts
• Home Screen widgets
• Light and dark mode, four colour themes

Your API key stays in the iPhone's Keychain. The app talks only to Scaleway: no servers, no analytics.

Source code (MPL 2.0): github.com/MoatCast/scaleway-companion

Independent project, not affiliated with Scaleway.
